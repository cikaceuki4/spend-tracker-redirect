Spend Tracker – privacy and terms

Spend Tracker is a personal, non-commercial application used only by its owner to view the owner's own bank accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy

  • Access to bank data is read-only and requires the owner's consent at the bank.
  • Account data is retrieved through Enable Banking and stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted. It is not shared, sold or sent anywhere else.
  • This website stores nothing and uses no cookies or analytics. Its /callback/ page only returns the owner's browser to the application on the owner's computer.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time at the bank or by deleting the application's data.

Terms

For the owner's personal use only. Provided as is, without warranty.
